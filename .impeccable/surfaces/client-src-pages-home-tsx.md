# Surface brief: public home page

Scope: `/` in client/src/pages/Home.tsx (public, signed-out and signed-in). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: students and young people in Hong Kong who share costs with friends; internship reviewers trying the product.
Job: understand VaultX in one screen, then open an account (primary) or log in.
Proof: real mechanisms only (atomic transfers, row locks, bcrypt, last-four card storage, 15-minute sessions); no invented customers, rates, ratings or licences.
Constraint: the owner pinned the genre: a retail bank home page alongside bankaust.com.au and halykbank.com, played straight at their craft level, not a copy of either.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A retail bank home page played straight, owned by one idea: paying friends back. Refuses the generic fintech hero of headline + app screenshot + logo row.
OWN-WORLD: Committed cobalt (#2341E0) owns the hero; the security band takes the brand night tone (#121C6B) so proof reads apart from offer; the close is the harbour-at-night photo under a deep-cobalt scrim behind its text; white and pale sky (#EEF1FF) sections between; ink #0B1020. Bricolage Grotesque display, Geist text. Real Hong Kong photography (tram, dim sum, Star Ferry, Mong Kok, harbour) with 20px radii; cards and phone drawn in HTML from the product's own UI.
STORY: Visitor sees the offer and tries a split, follows quick links, sees transfer by email, Hong Kong life in photos, card top-ups, the ledger, why money is safe, answers, then opens an account.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width cobalt hero under a white bank header (wordmark → /, five nav links, Log in, Open account). Left: two-line display headline, one sentence, Open an account + Log in. Right: tram photo, with a working "Split a bill" form overlapping its lower-left: total, people stepper, live per-person HK$ figure, CTA.
FORM: retail-bank canon, pinned by the user (named bankaust.com.au and halykbank.com); no concept-seed roll ran because a pinned direction beats the roll. seed: none (pinned)
SIGNATURE: the split calculator's per-person amount rolls to its new value on every change (from HK$0 on arrival); supporting motion: the phone mock plays a real transfer, the security steps light Lock → Move → Save in order, photos drift and unmask on scroll, cards fan in, statement rows arrive in order. Loops pause off screen; reduced-motion shows the settled state.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
